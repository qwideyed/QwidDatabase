Full-Stack Architecture & Pipeline

Technical requirements for media extraction backends

1. Cross-Origin (CORS) & Direct Stream Fetching

Web browsers restrict direct cross-origin video downloads when websites lack permissive Access-Control-Allow-Origin headers. A production backend (typically Node.js, Go, or Python) functions as an authenticated proxy to negotiate content-range streaming.

2. Video & Audio Remuxing via FFmpeg

High-resolution modern web players stream video (H.264/AV1/VP9) and audio (AAC/Opus) on completely separated adaptive bitrates (DASH / HLS). The server uses ffmpeg -i video.mp4 -i audio.m4a -c copy output.mp4 to merge them into a single container without costly re-encoding.

3. Terms of Service & Fair Use Standards

Always respect copyright laws, creative commons terms, and the Terms of Service of content platforms. This interface is tailored for public-domain footage, open creative archives, personal media backups, and permissible fair use workflows.
